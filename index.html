AltRA-Test An Alternative Annotator Test for LLM Judges Without Shared Annotators

Nagham Omar · Rotem Dror · Almog Gueta · Avigdor Gal · Roi Reichart · Nitay Calderon

Paper Data

Tests that decide whether an LLM can replace a human annotator need the same annotators to label every item, which crowdsourced and preference data rarely have. AltRA-Test resamples labels inside each item, swaps one for the LLM's, and asks whether the mixed panel's agreement stays within a budget set by the humans' own disagreement. It is a consistent test of asymptotic level α. Across 18 LLMs and 20 text, image, audio and video datasets, judges pass in 95 of 201 pairs with their best prompt, and about 100 items often suffice.
